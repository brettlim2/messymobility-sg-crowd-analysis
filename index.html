How to read this

A spike is a ~150 m geohash cell whose share of the island-wide panel, in a given hour, rose far above its own same-hour-of-week baseline (robust z ≥ 5, ≥ 50 devices, ≥ 3× typical). Adjacent cell-hours are clustered into one event and the window trimmed to the longest contiguous burst. The time-series chart shows that day's hourly crowd against the typical same-weekday band. Composition and origin cover home-anchored devices only (see each card's coverage); lift compares the crowd to the island average. Confidence is honest:
